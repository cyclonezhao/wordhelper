1. 穿黑色外套的那个男人是我的老师。
The man who is wearing a black coat is my teacher.
2. 她是我在派对上遇到的女孩。
She is the girl whom I met at the party.
3. 手机被偷的男孩大哭起来。
The boy whose phone was stolen cried loudly.
4. 这是那本改变我人生的书。
This is the book which/that changed my life.
5. 我上周买的笔记本电脑速度很快。
The laptop (which/that) I bought last week is very fast. -- 关系代词 which/that 在从句中作宾语时可省略。
6. 我仍记得我们初次见面的那一天。
I still remember the day when we first met.
7. 这是我出生的医院。
This is the hospital where I was born.
8. 告诉我你迟到的原因。
Tell me the reason why you were late.
9. 我们昨天看的那部电影很无聊。
The movie that we watched yesterday was boring.
10. 我哥哥是一名医生，他住在巴黎。
My brother, who lives in Paris, is a doctor.
11. 我长大的那栋房子被卖掉了。
The house in which I grew up was sold. -- 正式文体中可用 "in which" 代替 "where"。
12. 和我一起旅行的朋友是意大利人。
The friend with whom I traveled is Italian. -- that 可替代 who/which，但不能用于非限制性从句或介词后
13. 是约翰昨天打碎了花瓶。
It was John that/who broke the vase yesterday.
14. 是一阵突然的噪音吓到了我。
It was a sudden noise that scared me.
15. 她爱的是巧克力蛋糕。
It is chocolate cake that she loves.
16. 我在公园遇到的是你妹妹。
It was your sister that/who I met at the park.
17. 他是上周一去的纽约。
It was last Monday that he left for New York.
18. 他们是在这个洞穴里找到宝藏的。
It was in this cave that they found the treasure.
19. 是因为疼痛她才哭的。
It was because of the pain that she cried.
20. 他是通过使用AI解决了这个问题。
It was by using AI that he solved the problem.
21. 是在她避开我眼神时，我意识到她在撒谎。
It was when she avoided my eyes that I realized she was lying.
22. 我在点菠萝炒饭配鸡肉。关于喝的，我要一小杯柠檬水。一共应该是12美元左右。
I'm ordering the pineapple fried rice with chicken. And to drink, I'm having a small lemonade. The total should be about 12 dollars.
23. 我的父母从来没有让我做家务，所以我通常在周末休息
My parents have never made me do housework, so I often rest on the weekend.
24. 我真的很想吃金枪鱼三明治，但很少有餐馆能做出好的三明治。
I really want a tuna sandwich, but very few restaurants make good sandwiches.
25. 我很惊讶她变得如此外向
I'm surprised that she's become so outgoing.
26. 他是否会来仍然未知
Whether he will come is still unknown.
27. 真相是她之前很害羞
The truth is that she was shy before.
28. 她很外向这一事实使每个人都很惊讶
The fact that she is outgoing amazes everyone.
29. 当她进入房间时，所有人都笑了
When she entered the room, everyone smiled.
30. 她很高兴，因为她交了新朋友
She's happy because she made new friends.
31. 如果下雨，我们会呆在家
If it rains, we'll stay home.
32. 尽管她很害羞，她说话很自信
Although she was shy, she spoke confidently.
33. 她学习很努力，所以她能通过考试
She studies hard so that she can pass the exam.
34. 她说得如此清楚以至于每个人都明白了
She spoke so clearly that everyone understood.
35. 他的举动显得他知道一切
He acted as if he knew everything.
36. 按我给你展示的方式做
Do it the way I showed you.
37. 她比我高
She's taller than I (am)
38. 他跑得像猎豹一样快
He runs as fast as a cheetah.
39. 不仅风大，而且下起雨来了
Not only was it windy, but it (also) started to rain.
40. 它不仅起风而且下雨
It was not only windy but also rainy.
41. 风非常大，此外，开始下雨了
It was very windy. Besides, it began to rain.
42. 风非常大，此外，开始下雨了
The wind was strong. Moreover, rain started falling.
43. 有风，并且甚至开始下雨了
It was windy, and it even started raining.
44. 有强风携带雨
There was strong wind as well as rain.
45. 我们不得不取消野餐，因为不仅起风而且下雨
We had to cancel the picnic because not only was it windy, but it also rained
46. Ugh，它不仅起风，甚至正在下雨
Ugh, it's not just windy —— it's even raining now.
